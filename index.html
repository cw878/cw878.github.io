Pillar 01

Phoenix Capital

Phoenix Capital assesses submitted projects for financial and ESG bankability, then matches them to the most appropriate funding route — with a stated preference for public sector co-investment over pure private equity exit structures.

Submitted Project → Bankability & ESG Assessment → Public / Private Co-Investment

Bankability by Design

Evaluates campus decarbonisation and climate tech assets with financial QA, ESG indexing, and climate risk profiling.

Public Sector Grant Matching

Routes qualifying projects toward PSDS, Salix Finance, and Scottish Government Net Zero funding streams.

Funding Deployment

Prepares bankable investment cases for Scottish Enterprise, the UK Infrastructure Bank, and co-investment funds.

Phoenix Capital's scoring methodology and academic/research partnerships are in development. Any claimed third-party or university affiliation will be named here once formally confirmed — not before.

Pillar 02

Phoenix CleanCloud

An enterprise AI gateway built on an active AWS Bedrock infrastructure engagement — giving organisations unified, governed access to leading AI models without managing multiple provider relationships directly.

Universal API Gateway

Routes access to Anthropic Claude, Meta Llama, and specialised models tailored for energy modelling and climate analytics.

Enterprise Control & Guardrails

Token routing, usage telemetry, and delegated security guardrails for high-scale enterprise workloads.

Domain AI Micro-Models

Optimised endpoints for building management system telemetry, regulatory compliance, and climate adaptation simulations.

CleanCloud's gateway infrastructure is being built on our existing AWS Bedrock engagement — current scope covers Anthropic model access, with multi-provider routing (Llama and others) as a planned extension, not yet live.
