Four Core Platforms

Phoenix CleanTech's offer centres on four core platforms, connected by a single shared data layer, plus an AI & Data extension layer:

◈
Phoenix Platform

Funding & Grant Application Platform

Structured, AI-assisted preparation and submission of funding applications.

◉
Phoenix Platform

Automated Financial Modelling Platform

Ingests project data directly from a client's data room to generate investment-grade financial performance outputs.

◐
Phoenix Platform

Procurement & Supplier Bidding Platform

Connects to a supplier portal for tender invitation and bid management.

◇
Phoenix Platform

VPP Platform

Aggregates distributed generation and flexible demand across a community for dispatch, trading, and value-sharing.

The AI Mechanism: AI is not a bolt-on feature across these platforms — it is the mechanism that makes each one viable at public sector and community budgets. Manual equivalents of financial modelling, document drafting, and generation forecasting exist today, but require specialist consultants at costs this client base cannot sustain at scale. AI is what makes these platforms affordable and fast enough to be genuinely adopted.
