Funding & grant application platform

Structured, repeatable tooling to identify, prepare and submit funding and grant applications for renewables, retrofit and storage projects — reducing the time and specialist cost of each bid.

Councils · RSLs · Communities

Automated financial modelling platform

Pulls project information directly from your data room to generate investment-grade financial performance for investors and project owners — built to withstand committee and funder scrutiny.

Councils · RSLs · Investors

Procurement & supplier bidding platform

Councils · RSLs · IPPs

VPP platform

Virtual power plant software that aggregates distributed generation and flexible demand across a community, so local groups can dispatch, trade and share the value of nearby generation directly.
